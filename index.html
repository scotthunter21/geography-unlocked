Geography Unlocked

Geography Unlocked is a YouTube channel making short animated map videos about countries, borders, islands and the strange geography behind them.

The channel's owner uses a small private tool to upload their own videos to the Geography Unlocked YouTube channel as unlisted drafts and to read that channel's own view and audience statistics. The tool is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to anyone else.
